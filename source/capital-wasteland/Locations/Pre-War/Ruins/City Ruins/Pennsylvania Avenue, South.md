**Pennsylvania Avenue, South**
The large plaza still stands, despite proximity to such high value targets. It is currently a battlefield hosting BOS, Supermutants, Talon Company and Enclave forces, many finding strange bedfellows as the war has waged far past any sensible duration and has spiraled out of control.
