**Supreme Court**
This place once represented the communal efforts of the commonwealths to vie over one another in attempt to strengthen America's future. Clearly something went very wrong in that process as despite, or perhaps because of their efforts the opposite outcome clearly occurred. Strangely enough a number of robots are repairing the old structure at the behest of the current president.
