L.O.B. Enterprises
L.O.B. Enterprises was a pre-War weapons manufacturer before the war. Rumors arose and subsequently spread of their activities, and L.O.B. Enterprises soon came under federal scrutiny, due to importing Chinese products from Happy Liberty Imports, a Chinese front.
