**Warrington Station**
This metro station resides in an open area within eye sight of Tenpenny tower and is relatively unblemished from the war. The interior itself seems to have been a makeshift neighborhood for a number of now absent settlers. A painted sign pointing westward directs readers towards the attached trainyard saying "The Revolution Starts here" in fresh paint.
