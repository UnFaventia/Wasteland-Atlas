**Metro Station, Jury Street**
This once lively neighborhood escaped the immediate fallout of the war, but not its aftermath. The now despoiled carcass of a neighborhood now resembled a warzone. A covered stairway leads down into the metro tunnels, they survived relatively unscathed, but are likely occupied.
