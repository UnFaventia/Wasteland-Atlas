**Seward Square Metro**
The metro itself is in relatively good condition, its interior being used heavily after the war has left it in some disrepair. The exterior is currently a battlefield between Talon Company mercs and super mutants over the Capitol Building has spilled out to this are. To the east near the subway station a number of signs point towards "Reilly's Rangers" while occasional Enclave Vertibirds drop robotic forces into the fray and offer air support.
