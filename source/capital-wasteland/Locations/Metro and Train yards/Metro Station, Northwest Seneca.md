**Metro Station, Northwest Seneca**
While still accessible from ground level the interior of the metro has suffered great damage. This collapsed metro station has an irradiated sewer tunnel that leads to Meresti service tunnel and eventually Meresti Metro station. A strong chemical small most would associate with drugs eliminate from within.
