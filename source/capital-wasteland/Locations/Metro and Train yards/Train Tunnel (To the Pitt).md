**Train Tunnel (To the Pitt)**
This once train tunnel was converted into a makeshift camp so slavers can periodically sell slaves to the Pitt raiders. A few shacks and number of overturned boxcars have been retrofitted to hold their chattel. The Absolut darkness of the long tunnel houses little more than an old hand cart.
