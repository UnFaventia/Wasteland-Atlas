**Flooded Metro**
The flooded metro is located on the side of an old expressway which is littered with decrepit pre-War cars and runs under the rubble of a highway overpass. The interior of the flooded metro is a fairly long and winding maze of stairs and tunnels that connect to Mason District South at the other end.
