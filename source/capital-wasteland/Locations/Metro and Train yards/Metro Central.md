**Metro Central**
Metro Central was once the main D.C. Metro hub, the largest metro station in the region and likely the most frequented pre-war. Due to its strategic importance, it has been the battleground of several skirmishes between raiders the Talon Company and the feral ghouls. The aftermath of these skirmishes have left several members' corpses of said factions scattered all around the station, with two barricades still left standing.
