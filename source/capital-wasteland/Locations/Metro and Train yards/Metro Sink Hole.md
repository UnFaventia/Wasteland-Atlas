**Metro Sink Hole**
A massive sinkhole has opened in the earth, proving potentially convenient access to the metro system. Likely started by an untouched and still running burst water main, the last 200 years has formed a hole large enough to connect to a nearby, now partly flooded cave system that it has formed and the metro that it has connected to.
