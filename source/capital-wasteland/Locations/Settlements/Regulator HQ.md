**Regulator HQ**
More of a meeting place for do-gooders and law enforcing types, this large farmhouse serves as the current meeting place for the Regulators. Those desperate enough to seek justice or those looking to collect bounties on their behalf usually arrive here. Beyond that a small farm has been erected, likely due to the relative security its reputation provides, at least against ne'er-do-wells.
