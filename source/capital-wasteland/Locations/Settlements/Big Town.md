**Big Town**
The population of Big Town is mostly teenagers and young adults that were formerly residents of Little Lamplight, having been expelled upon their sixteenth birthday. The now heavily fortified settlement has hit hard times as the super mutants have turned their eyes towards it for victims to kidnap.
