**Underworld**
A large stairwell leads from the Mall to the once Museum of History has been occupied and made a settlement by a number of none feral, ostracized ghouls. The settlement of Underworld was founded circa 2230 out of the necessity for ghouls to live their lives in relative peace. They are wary of strangers, but will extend a civil welcome to all visitors so long as they behave themselves. The town gets its name from a special exhibit of the Museum of History that was being displayed there in late October 2077, showcasing the various cultural interpretations of the afterlife held by different civilizations throughout history.
