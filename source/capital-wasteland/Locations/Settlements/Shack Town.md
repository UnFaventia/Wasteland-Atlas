**Shack Town**
The village is a mishmash of rusted shacks and derelict vehicles while the southernmost edge of the village is protected by a steep cliff. Its inhabitants happily serve as a way station and hub of trade. It has abruptly gone quiet in recent times however, but that isn't unprecedented.
