**Evergreen Mills**
The once forested hills of the area were torn away far before the end of the world leaving it a highly defensible mined out box canyon. The factory at its heart having plunder the earth for its old world operations is now the HQ of the Evergreen hills raiders. While opportunistic raiders and slavers, they do welcome travels to pursue their services, as long as they have caps to spend. Much of there business as of late has been split between supplying the slavers of the Pitt with fresh bodies and more recently, the local Supermutants.
