**The Citadel**
The pre-war Pentagon is now the acting HQ for the Brotherhood of Steel. After the Scourging of the Pitt and arrival to DC, they renovated and occupied the ruins. The Citadel has now become unassailable by almost any factions. The Brotherhood now directs its forces against the Super Mutant hoards and newly its water distribution caravans.
