**The Raven Rock Bunker**
Now serving as the headquarters for President Eden and Purists faction of the Enclave, its exterior is otherwise featureless. A long since detonated parking lot sits filled to capacity, a small path leading to a massive metal door sunken into concrete and stone lays beyond.
