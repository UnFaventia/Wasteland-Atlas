**Sander's Gambit**
Sander’s party founded this settlement after a falling out with fellow ghouls and being rejected by Tenpenny Tower. Sanders' group of ghouls wanted to establish a new settlement away from the bigotry of the wasteland in Old Olney. The overwhelming abundance of Deathclaws in the area was seemingly a blessing as the ruins have been left relatively untouched and raider free and has allowed them to make a home of the Old Olney underground. This security however comes with great risk as it would take little more than an accident for it to be turned inwards.
