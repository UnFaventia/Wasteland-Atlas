**Mama Dolce's**
Originally a pre-war front for Chinese spies and the People's Liberation Army, this business found rapid and unexpected success selling what could be generously considered food to pre-war Americans. To this day many of the workers that used printer ink and sawdust as food additives still remain within, having become ghouls. Still desiring to convert the masses to communism, they will attempt to sway wastelands towards their beliefs as long as they are not hostile.
