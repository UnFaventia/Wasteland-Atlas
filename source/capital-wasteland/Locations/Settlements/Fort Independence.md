**Fort Independence**
The Outcasts have adapted the old fort to their needs and restored much of its internal facilities, using it as a new HQ. All of the technology they gather is stored within along with their research findings. Originally belonging to the raiders of Fairfax, the Outcasts easily expropriated the facility, though it remains under near constant attack by its former residents.
