**Mobile Base Crawler**
The current HQ of Colonel Autumn's Reformist faction of the Enclave, having been recently relocated from the nearby Adams Airforce Base, was pushed to its limits. The heavily armed and armored platform acts as a sort of mobile bunker and is currently hooked into Ole Olney's power supplies via power stations as its reactor undergoes repairs.
