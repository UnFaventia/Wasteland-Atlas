**Andale**
This portion of pre-war suburbia and its population have seemingly become dead set on keeping pre-war America alive. The all-American lifestyle of Andale's residents is not an act either; they have lived this way ever since the Great War. During the war, their four founding families hid in their fallout shelters beneath Andale, including their descendants who still remain.
