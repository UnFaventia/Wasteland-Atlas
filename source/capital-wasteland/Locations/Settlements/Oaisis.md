**Oasis**
First founded on top of a number of old-world mining tunnels, it has been transformed by unnatural greenery. A verdant forest has expanded from an epicenter at its tucked away and isolated  core. The Treeminder cult soon established itself and now protects their grove while seeking to recruit like-minded individuals.
