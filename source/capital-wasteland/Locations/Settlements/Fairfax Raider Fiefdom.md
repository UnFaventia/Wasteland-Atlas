**Fairfax Raider Fiefdom**
Originally based in Fort Independence, these raiders were displaced by the Outcasts and have now taken over the utility tunnels under the town. Loosely organized towards raiding over slaving like their fellows in Evergreen Mills and Paradise Falls. These ruthless killers only respect strength and often cannibalize their victims. While uncommon, some may barter or offer services to those who are too much trouble to fight, have something to offer and or on good terms.
