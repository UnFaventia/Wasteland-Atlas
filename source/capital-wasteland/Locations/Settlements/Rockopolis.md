**Rockopolis**
A rumored to be hidden, subterranean kingdom led by its isolationist leader, King Crag. It now offers shelter for a variety of outcasts that would be otherwise turned away by the wider world. Recent rumors and boasts however, say that it was cleared out to a man by the raiders of Evergreen Mills.
