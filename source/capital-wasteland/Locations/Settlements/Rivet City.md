**Rivet City**
The once pre-war aircraft carrier now serves as one of the safest and oldest settlements in the Capital Wasteland. Having been beach soon after the world ended, it has remained in place ever since and hosted an entire community within its guts. Its reactor remains the envy of the Brotherhood of Steel and allowed for the original investment in Project Purity.
