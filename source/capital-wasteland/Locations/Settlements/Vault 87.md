**Vault 87**
This once Vault now serves as the birthplace of most of the Supermutants in DC, if not all of them. Tyrannically lead by the once gentile Fawkes, he was driven mad after the Lone Wanderer left him to his fate. He now has embraced the cruelty of his brethren to a point where he leads unchallenged. Protected by both isolation and impassible radiation, the mutants of Vault 87 hoard their pilfered gear and and plan there search for "green stuff".
