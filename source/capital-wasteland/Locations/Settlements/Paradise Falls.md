**Paradise Falls**
A pre-war shopping center turned fortified slaver den, all overlooked by an ice cream welding giant in suspenders of plaid. Now the HQ for the Capital Wasteland's slavers, it sees great wealth and suffering pass through. Those willing to behave may even peruse the market or take advantage of its many services.
