**Little Lamplight**
Originally a tourist destination, this cave system has become known as a dumping ground for orphans. Apparently populated exclusively by children since it's unfortunate school trip founding, all “Mongos” are banned entry and once you've reached the ripe age of 16, you are banished from the settlement.
