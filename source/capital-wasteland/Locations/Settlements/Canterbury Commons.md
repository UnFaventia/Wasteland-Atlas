**Canterbury Commons**
A popular trading settlement recently founded by Ernest "Uncle" Roe and his sister Daisy as a trading center for caravan merchants in the Capital Wasteland. Recently it has become embroiled in an abnormal conflict between two “super hero” themed individuals; the AntAgonizer and "the Mechanist".
