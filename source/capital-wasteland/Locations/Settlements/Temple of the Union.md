**Temple of the Union**
The Temple of the Union is a small compound in the remains of a derelict building that serves as the headquarters of the Abolitionists, a group of escaped slaves who wish to create a haven to protect and inspire all runaway slaves. A shrine of sorts has been erected around the old world god of freedom Abraham Lincoln, as his statue's head resides at its center, although it is unclear how it came to be here.
