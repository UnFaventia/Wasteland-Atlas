**Vault 101**
One of the few pre-war vaults built before the war, 101 only recently came into broader recognition as its Lone Wander activated Project Purity prior to their death. Historically It has only ever been rumored to have opened twice, but some have recently reported Enclave forces attempting to access the great vault door, seemingly with little success.
