**Fort Bannister**
Now serving as the Talon Company HQ, it was originally a pre-war installation that was targeted directly during the great-war. This strike left the underground bunker exposed, but still intact with little long lasting damage. Willing to take payment from anyone for anything, these merciless mercenaries are well funded and could be potentially convinced to barter with active patrons.
