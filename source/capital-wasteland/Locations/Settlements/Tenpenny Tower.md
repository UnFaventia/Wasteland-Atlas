**Tenpenny Tower**
This once pre-war resort for the wealthy and powerful has been fortified and preserved fairly well over the years. It now serves as one of the safest, cleanest and all together most desirable places in the capital wastelands to live. Tenpenny Tower had become a private and elitist residential and commercial building owned by Allistair Tenpenny, an entrepreneur from England who only permits the well-to-do or wealthy to reside there.
