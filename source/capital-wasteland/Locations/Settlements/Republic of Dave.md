**Republic of Dave**
A small farming settlement in the far northeast of the Capital Wasteland, that is considered by its close-knit inhabitants to be a sovereign nation-state. In earlier years it was known as the Kingdom of Tom and Billsylvania before being taken over by its current ruler, Dave, who subsequently allowed voting, and thus, it became a republic.
