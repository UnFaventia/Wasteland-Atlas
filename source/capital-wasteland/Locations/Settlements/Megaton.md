**Megaton**
Originally built by survivors attempting to enter Vault 101, its founders took shelter in a deep crater created by a still active, but un-detonated warhead. Over the past 200+ years it was built up greatly, now having a massive turbine raised gate, shanty buildings climbing the crater walls and a large scrap metal wall around the settlement.
